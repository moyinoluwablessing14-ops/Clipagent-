Privacy

What we receive

The YouTube link you submit and the video type you pick. There are no accounts, and we do not ask for your name or email.

Who else sees it

The link is sent to Google's Gemini service so it can watch the video and suggest clips. Google handles it under its own terms. Videos you link to are played from YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your light or dark setting is saved in your browser. Clear your browser data to remove it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising on Kairos.

Questions

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
